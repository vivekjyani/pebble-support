01 · NOTICE

Keep your first moment

Tap place a stone, write a few words, and add a photo if you like. Tap place this stone to add it to your cairn.

02 · REMEMBER

Find something good

Tap any stone to read it again. Use the books button to browse your memories, search for a word, or choose all moments and filter by date.

03 · KEEP

Save a backup

Open the books button, then the gear button. Choose save a backup and a destination in Files. Your backup includes your words, dates, and photos. Save a fresh copy after adding more moments.

04 · RETURN

Bring moments back

In the same settings page, choose restore a backup and select a Pebble backup. Restoring adds missing moments and preserves the ones already saved. Backups aren’t password protected; keep them somewhere you trust.

A few common questions.

Why is “on this day” empty?

It shows moments saved on the same month and day in earlier years. A new collection will have none yet. Choose “all moments” to see everything you’ve saved.

How do reminders work?

Open the memory library with the books button, then tap the gear. Turn on “on this day reminders” and allow notifications when asked. They are optional, off by default, and scheduled at 9am on anniversary dates. A generic alert invites you back; your private words and photos are not included. Check Pebble’s notification permission in your device Settings if alerts don’t appear.

Can I change or remove a memory?

Open the stone, tap its menu, and choose edit or delete. Editing keeps the original date and photo. Deletion asks for confirmation. Copies in earlier exported backups are separate and aren’t changed.

Does Pebble sync between devices?

Pebble stores each collection locally. It doesn’t automatically sync between your iPhone and iPad. Export a backup from one device and restore it on the other to transfer saved moments.

What if a photo or backup won’t save?

Check available device storage and that the selected photo is available on your device. For a backup, try saving to a folder you can access in Files. Retry, and email support with the exact error if the problem continues.

Can you recover a lost collection?

We don’t receive or store your memories on a server, so we can’t retrieve them for you. If you have a Pebble backup, restore it in the app. An existing device backup may also contain app data, depending on your device’s backup settings.

Still need a hand?

Email vivekjyani11@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Pebble version, and what happened.

Please don’t send private journal entries, personal photos, or backup files. A description of the issue is usually enough.
