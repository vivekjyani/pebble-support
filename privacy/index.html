Privacy policy · Updated November 22, 2026

Your moments
are yours.

Pebble stores your memories on your device. No account, no advertising, and no analytics.

What the app collects

Pebble does not collect your personal data or send your memories to a server. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Your words and photos

Your memory text, dates, and attached photos are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. The app and its home-screen widget use a private shared app container to access the saved data.

When you choose a photo using Apple’s system photo picker, Pebble receives only the image you select. It resizes and saves a local copy. Pebble does not request access to your entire photo library, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Backups and device transfers

You can export a backup containing your words, dates, and attached photos. You choose its destination in Files, which may be on your device or a storage service you use. Sharing or uploading that file is your choice, and the destination’s own privacy practices apply.

Exported backups are not password protected. Keep them in storage you trust.

Restoring a backup adds missing memories without replacing existing ones. Pebble does not provide automatic cloud synchronization.

Your device’s operating-system backup service, such as iCloud Backup, may include Pebble’s local data depending on your settings. This is separate from any backup initiated inside Pebble.

Optional anniversary reminders

Anniversary reminders are off until you enable them and grant notification permission. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Pebble does not use a remote notification server or collect a push token.

Alerts contain a generic invitation to revisit a memory, not your saved words or photos. You can disable reminders in Pebble or manage notification permission in your device Settings.

Editing and deleting memories

You can edit a memory’s words or delete the memory in the app. An attached photo is removed from the app’s local storage when no remaining memory uses it.

Previously exported backups and operating-system backups are separate copies. Deleting a memory in Pebble does not change those copies; manage them with the storage provider or device settings you use.

Children’s privacy

Pebble does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no public social feed, chat, advertisements, or purchases.

Support messages and this website

If you email vivekjyani11@gmail.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private memories, personal photos, or backup files.

This support website has no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or embedded third-party content. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub privacy statement. Email services also process support correspondence under their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if Pebble’s privacy practices change.

For privacy questions, email vivekjyani11@gmail.com.
